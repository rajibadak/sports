About Delta Sports Emporium

Delta Sports Emporium is a one-stop shop for sports equipment and accessories. The stall serves schools, clubs, coaches and individual players, with the same gear available at wholesale and retail prices.

The shop is Stall No. 3 at Satish Samanta Marketing Complex, Mahishadal, Purba Medinipur, West Bengal. Customers come in for cricket, football, tennis, badminton, boxing, accessories and trophies, including MRF, Cosco, Yonex, Dunlop, Nivia, SS, adidas and Puma.

  • Wide collection of sports items
  • Best prices in wholesale and retail
  • Trusted by athletes, schools and clubs
  • Stock and sizes confirmed in the shop
